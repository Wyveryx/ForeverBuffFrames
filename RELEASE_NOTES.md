# ForeverBuffFrames 0.9.10-beta.1

Phase 13 completes the tracking selector and the pre-publish interface pass.

## Highlights

- Adds one fixed magnifying-glass selector at the physical far right of the buff bar.
- Activates supported profession, racial, and class tracking through native secure spell actions.
- Displays active tracking effects as ordinary buffs instead of duplicating them in a second tracking slot.
- Keeps the selector inside the movable buff-frame outline and aligned with the configured icon size.
- Improves dropdown spacing, layering, containment, and long-label behavior throughout the settings window.
- Adds current tracking-selector and ten-second expiration-watch details to Diagnostics.
- Removes routine startup and successful test debug messages from chat while retaining actionable failures.

## Alert media

The package includes 21 redistributable sounds selected from SharedMedia: AlertMediaPack 1.0.2. Their original CC0, CC BY 3.0, MIT, and upstream attribution documents are included under `Media/AlertSounds/Licenses` and summarized in `Media/AlertSounds/NOTICE.md`.

## Testing status

The Phase 13 release gate covers expiration and removal alerts, debuff sounds and Personal Trackers, cross-character profiles and recovery, configurable aura layouts, Diagnostics, tracking activation, and the pre-publish interface review.

WoW Forever remains in beta. Please include the client build and the complete Diagnostics report with reproducible bug reports.
