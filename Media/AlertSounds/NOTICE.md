# Bundled alert sounds

ForeverBuffFrames includes 21 audio files sourced from **SharedMedia: AlertMediaPack 1.0.2**:

- Project: https://github.com/WSakhana/SharedMedia_AlertMediaPack
- Author: Anahkas / Alert Media Pack contributors

Only recordings with explicit CC0 1.0, CC BY 3.0, or MIT terms were copied. The seven files described by the source project only as “Anahkas-provided alert sounds” were intentionally excluded because their downstream redistribution permission was not explicit.

The original attribution table and applicable license texts are preserved in the adjacent `Licenses` directory. CC BY recordings must retain their listed creator attribution when redistributed.
